# Notice: Brain Loom demo (x3d.brain)

**Not a medical device. Not for diagnosis, treatment or any clinical decision.** This folder is a demonstration and a learning project: a high-fidelity brain scene, a wider-medical CT torso scene, and the Loom witnesses that check them. Looks come first and physical accuracy second, and nothing in it is allowed to pretend otherwise.

This notice is the complete statement of what the shipped files are made from, under what terms, what we changed, and what was generated by a model. The shorter notices `NOTICE-BRAIN-DATA.md` (brain) and `data/TORSO-NOTICE.md` (torso) stay as they are and agree with this one.

## 1. What is real, what is synthetic, what is generated

| Thing | Status | Where it says so |
|---|---|---|
| Cortex surface shapes | Real template anatomy (an average brain, not a person): the template's own lower-density vertex sets (10k lite, 41k full), moved rigidly, not decimated by us | WorldInfo `sources:` and `geometry:` |
| Deep structures (thalamus, caudate, putamen, pallidum, hippocampus, amygdala, accumbens, ventricles, brain stem, cerebellum) | Real template segmentation, meshed and smoothed | WorldInfo `sources:` |
| T1 slices | The template's own T1-weighted image, windowed and resampled to six 2D pictures | WorldInfo `slice window:` |
| Alignment of the cortex to the deep structures | **Approximate**: the deep structures and slices are scaled and shifted per axis (3 to 5 percent) to fit the cortex's bounding box; the cortex itself is not scaled (1.000). A look-level fit, not a registration | WorldInfo `ALIGNMENT NOTICE`, witness `data-true` |
| White-matter tracts | **SYNTHETIC**: arcs generated between region vertices, coloured by direction. Not tractography of any subject or scan | WorldInfo `SYNTHETIC NOTICE`, metadata on `TRACTS` |
| Activation blobs | **ILLUSTRATIVE**: placed glowing sprites. Not measured activity | WorldInfo, metadata on `SW_ACTIV` |
| Torso anatomy | Real segmentation of one CT subject, meshed (changes were made) | `data/TORSO-NOTICE.md`, torso WorldInfo |
| Brain inside the torso | **ILLUSTRATIVE**: the template brain fitted into the subject's head; the folds are the template's, not the subject's | torso WorldInfo |
| Glow, halos, glass, colours, smoke | Artistic choices, not tissue properties | both WorldInfo texts |
| Music beds, style-board pictures, atlas facts, tour and interface copy | **Generated by models** (section 7) | `ledger/`, this notice |

## 2. Derived only

The scenes contain **derived, low-polygon geometry and derived 2D pictures only**. No raw template volume, no full-resolution surface (the 164k fsaverage files), no raw CT or segmentation volume, and no style-board image is in this repository. `data/looks.json` holds palette numbers measured from the style-board images; it holds no pixels.

## 5. Change notes (changes were made)

- **Cortex:** the 10k (lite) or 41k (full) pial surfaces as TemplateFlow distributes them (lower-resolution versions of the 164k surface; we did not decimate them). The cortex is **not** scaled or fitted: its axes are reordered to x right, y up, z posterior (anterior is -z), the origin is moved to the pial bounding-box centre, and coordinates are printed to 0.1 mm. Triangles whose three vertices all carry the `unknown` or `corpuscallosum` (medial wall) label were removed so the deep structures can be seen from the medial side (lite: 37,112 of 40,960 triangles kept; the vertices stay). Normals recomputed; per-vertex ambient occlusion baked and stored in the vertex colour; labels turned into region centroids and label positions in `data/regions.json`.
- **Deep structures and T1 slices:** these are what is moved. The MNI152 `aseg` meshes and the T1 slices are carried from MNI space into the fsaverage frame by a per-axis scale and shift that makes the `aseg` cerebral-cortex bounding box equal the fsaverage pial bounding box (RAS x, y, z; lite build: scale 0.963, 0.966, 0.952; shift 0.5, -1.3, 1.1 mm; the full 41k build fits slightly differently: scale 0.963, 0.968, 0.953; shift 0.5, -1.1, 1.1 mm). These per-axis scales apply to the deep meshes and the slices only; the cortex stays at 1.000 on every axis. Meshes: Gaussian smoothing of each label mask, marching cubes (a coarser step in the lite build; there is no separate decimation pass), Taubin smoothing of the mesh (lite: 352 to 1,944 vertices per structure). The `ventricles` mesh joins the `aseg` labels 4, 43 (lateral ventricles), 5, 44 (inferior lateral ventricles), 14 (third ventricle) and 15 (fourth ventricle); it is named "Ventricular system" everywhere (region data, scene labels, facts, tour, page).
- **T1 slices:** six axial slices at 1.1 mm per pixel, intensity window from the 2nd to the 99.7th percentile of non-zero voxels, gamma 0.85, opacity from intensity. The window and the clipped fractions are declared in WorldInfo: of the tissue voxels on the six slices 0.31% clip at the top, 5.04% clip at the bottom (they draw as the darkest colour) and 5.64% fall under the alpha ramp and are fully transparent.
- **Torso:** each binary segmentation converted to a signed-distance field, marching cubes, Taubin smoothing, quadric decimation; skin from a CT threshold (HU above -300, largest component, holes filled); lung vessels from a CT iso-surface (HU above -470, on the CT smoothed with a 0.6-voxel Gaussian) inside lung masks eroded by one voxel, which includes airway walls; the template brain (the cortex is decimated here, to about 3,000 vertices per hemisphere in the lite scene and 7,000 in the full one; the cerebellum and brain stem likewise) affinely fitted into the subject's brain mask by ICP (mean two-way surface distance 10.30 mm; the fit stretches the template by 1.115, 1.088 and 1.070 along its principal axes, volume x1.30, so millimetres on the brain ghost are not the template's millimetres). The subject's forward head posture and raised arms are shown as recorded.
- **Look:** glow, halos, glass fresnel, translucency and colour are rendering choices.

## 7. Generated content disclaimer

- **Music:** two instrumental tracks (each about 33 seconds (32.77 s of audio), complete clips; the shipped files are re-encoded ogg/mp3 with true durations (the source WAV headers over-declared 65.5 s)) were generated by a music model from text prompts. They contain no sampled recordings that we know of; we make no claim of human authorship and no claim about rights beyond what the generating service allows. Check its terms before reusing them.
- **Style board:** twelve pictures generated by an image model were used inside the project as visual targets. They are **not** in this repository. They are pictures of imagined brains, not data.
- **Atlas facts, tour text and interface copy:** drafted with a text model, then rewritten and checked against standard neuroanatomy (`data/facts.json` says so in its own notice). All 36 entries that came from the model draft were reworded afterwards and 8 of the 44 entries were written after it; nobody with neuroanatomy or clinical training has reviewed the text. Model text enters this project as grade S: it can be wrong, it is educational, and **no expert or clinical review is claimed**. It is not medical advice.
- **Region labels and function descriptions** describe a template atlas of a group average. They do not describe any person's brain.
- Every generation call is recorded in `ledger/LEDGER.public.jsonl`: the prompt as sent, the sha256 of what came back, seconds and token counts, with model names replaced by "image model", "text model" and "music model". `ledger/LEDGER.summary.json` holds counts and seconds. Costs, accounts and projects are not published. Rebuild both with `tools/public_ledger.py`.

## 8. How the claims are checked

```
x3d.brain/run_brain.sh runs/brain-20261106     # grammar, meaning, traps, data-true, data-true-controls; receipt; two controls that must fail; a last scan of the run folder for paths, hosts, vendor, model and cost words
python3 -I -B x3d.brain/tests/test_brain_truth.py   # the reader's own controls: a normal child and a python -O child, compared (it also runs when started with -O)
(cd x3d.brain && python3 -I -B tools/brain_scene_truth.py --strict --glob 'scenes/*.x3d')   # the glob is relative to the study folder
```

Accepting under `kit/profiles/brain-demo.json` means the scenes are valid, free of the known traps, internally consistent, drawn at load (every measured part is in a Switch child that is selected) and carry the SYNTHETIC, ILLUSTRATIVE and alignment notices; the reader looks for those words and cannot judge whether they are true. It says nothing about how the scenes look, how they run in a browser, or whether any position is anatomically right. The receipt is unsigned. `run_brain.sh` needs the x3d_mcp fork checkout with its own `.venv` (`LOOM_FORK`, or `~/Projects/x3d/x3d_mcp` under the `HOME` it runs with).

## 9. Known gaps

- Fsaverage-lineage rights are graded **Suspected**; a licence file for the TemplateFlow copy was not found.
- The torso subject's licence is **Likely**, not read.
- The alignment is a look-level fit; the witness catches gross misplacement only. One measurement of how close it is (a one-off script, not part of the witness; 60,000 random `aseg` cortex voxel centres carried through the shipped fit, distance to the nearest of the 20,484 vertices of the 10k pial mesh): median 2.2 mm, 95th percentile 4.9 mm, largest 10.2 mm; without the fit the 95th percentile is 5.4 mm. The two templates are different average brains and the mesh vertices are 3 to 4 mm apart, so this is a consistency figure, not a registration error.
- Thirty of 56,488 triangles in the lite brain scene (ventricle meshes) collapse to zero area at 0.1 mm coordinate precision; they draw nothing and are counted by the witness.
- The reader does not read material parameters (a glass opacity of 0, lights off, a viewpoint far away: the controls pin these as blind spots), does not require an extra line shape to be labelled, and checks the notices by their words.
- Eyes, behaviour, page code and real-GPU runs are recorded as not run.

To report a rights concern or an error in this notice, open an issue on the repository that carries it.
