# Field guide, batch 6 additions

The v5 guide (`FIELD-GUIDE-v5.md`) still stands. Grades: Confirmed / Likely / Suspected.

1. **The order of a method depends on where you measure it.** Symplectic Euler shows order 1.00 measured at T = 1 and 2.00 at one full period T = 2 pi; explicit Euler reads 1.10 there. The leading error term can cancel over a full period. Fit at a time that is not a multiple of the period. _(harmonic oscillator, 5 step sizes each; Confirmed)_
2. **Bounded energy is not accuracy.** On a Kepler orbit (e = 0.6, 200 steps per orbit) velocity Verlet keeps the energy error at 0.74% for 50 orbits with no growth (ratio 1.0000001) and is 0.61 (orbit radius units) off the true position after 5 orbits. RK4 drifts in energy (6.93e-05 to 3.35e-04) but is 0.0022 off: 283 times better. The phase error is the one that a picture shows. _(Kepler, exact position from Newton on Kepler's equation; Confirmed)_
3. **A symplectic method's advantage is asymptotic, and I did not measure the crossover.** At 200 steps per orbit RK4 beat Verlet on both energy (3.35e-04 against 7.35e-03) and position over 50 orbits. RK4's energy error was still rising when I stopped, so a crossing very likely exists later; I did not run to it. _(Kepler, 50 orbits; Suspected (the crossing))_
4. **Sweep along the long axis.** 200 boxes in a 4 x 1 field: sweeping x needs 1002 box tests per frame, sweeping y 4133 (4.1 times more), brute force 19900. Pick the axis with the largest spread. _(mean of 180 frames; Confirmed)_
5. **Sweep and prune is not O(n) at constant density.** Tests per frame: 355, 1010, 2878, 8076 for 100, 200, 400, 800 boxes at fixed density and aspect: exponent 1.50. The reason is that each box tests every box whose x range it overlaps, and that count grows with the field height. _(counted tests, 20 frames each; Confirmed (the exponent); Likely (the reason))_
6. **Sort by the left edge, not by the centre.** Sorting by centre with the usual early exit missed 905 overlapping pairs over 180 frames. A large box's left edge is far from its centre. _(same frames, brute-force pair sets; Confirmed)_
7. **Morton codes: test the bit trick against the slow loop, including the corners.** 100,008 inputs (random, all four corners, the alternating patterns 0xAAAA / 0x5555), 0 mismatches. _(naive bit-by-bit interleave; Confirmed)_
8. **A Morton-sorted tree gives log-like queries from one sort.** 4096 boxes, 1000 random queries: 78.6 tests per query against 4096 (1.92%), 0 queries different from brute force, about 5.2 hits each. 12 levels deep (log2 n = 12), so about 15 tests per hit. _(set equality with an O(n) search; Confirmed)_
9. **Node boxes must enclose their children, exactly.** Shrinking every node box by 0.02 lost 2466 hits over 1000 queries. A tree that is a little too tight is silently wrong, not slow. _(same queries; Confirmed)_
10. **A mutation control with a zero limit cannot fail.** My first 'limit x 10' edits on references whose limit is 0 (pairs missed, mismatches) changed nothing and the checker accepted them: two of eleven controls were vacuous. Pick a reference with a nonzero limit, and count the controls that were caught. _(found by running them, fixed in witness/mutate.py; Confirmed)_
