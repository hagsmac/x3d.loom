# Errata for batches 1 to 4, from an independent review (13 Nov 2026)

Four reviewers (one per batch) read the generator code and page text of v1 to v4 and tried to refute every claim by recomputing it themselves; a second, skeptical agent then tried to knock down each negative finding. **125 claims checked, 55 findings reported, 25 negative findings sent to verification, 25 survived, 0 false alarms.** The earlier folders are left exactly as merged (never overwrite a demo); this file is the correction. Grades: Confirmed means reproduced by the reviewer's own numbers.

## v1 (billiards, cannonball, bead)
1. **Billiards bug, new consequences (Confirmed).** v1 fired the impulse on separating pairs (6211 impulses in the rack, 131,418 approaching overlaps ignored). In a head-on pair the struck ball stayed at rest while the cue pushed it along for about 2 m (a bulldozer, not a collision). The cue crossed all five rows and reached the far cushion with 0.1% of the kinetic energy given to balls 1 to 15; the first real transfer (63%) came on the return at 1.8 s, an accident of overlap geometry. The v3 ERRATA phrase "no impulse applied" is imprecise: impulses were applied, to the wrong pairs.
2. **"Collisions conserve energy to 1.3e-15" is vacuous (Confirmed).** The same number results with the buggy rule (1.33e-15), the fixed rule (2.0e-15) and with no collisions at all (0). Any swap of normal components conserves kinetic energy whichever sign triggers it. The check does bite for restitution 0.99 (0.30). Duration is 14 s, not 15 s (v1 and v3 pages, ERRATA).
3. **Cannonball "grows without limit" is refuted (Confirmed).** Explicit Euler with mirror walls saturates at about 12.9 times E0 (13.16 at 3000 s, 12.77 at 9000 s). "Nearly doubles" is the 12 s snapshot. The outcome depends on the wall rule as well as the update order: with clamp-and-flip walls the symplectic ball decays to 0.16 E0 and the explicit one stays bounded. Correct statement: both orders drift by plus or minus g^2 h^2 / 2 per step in free flight, and the mirror-wall toll cancels the symplectic loss only on average, for this wall rule at this dt. "Every demo that follows updates velocity first" was not tested.
4. **"Chaotic, so late position error is bounded by box size" is refuted (Confirmed).** x and z motion is free flight with mirror reflection, exact for any dt (max error 2.3e-14). Only y differs, as a smooth phase drift. A single-instant error ranking is phase-aliased: the receipt makes explicit Euler look 3 times closer at 12 s, but RMS y error over the run is 0.79 (symplectic) against 1.14 (explicit). Use RMS or amplitude and period error.
5. **"Substeps beat iterations" (bead) is vacuous (Confirmed).** One constraint cannot show it: 1 vs 10 iterations at 1 substep differ by 5e-15. What the bead shows is that a smaller step loses less energy (100 substeps keep 93%).
6. **Bead "constraint drift < 2e-16" is vacuous (Confirmed).** It is measured after the projection, which sets the radius to rounding by construction. Pre-projection violation is 2.4e-2, 1.1e-3, 7.2e-5, 2.9e-6 for 1, 5, 20, 100 substeps; radial speed 0.203, 0.051, 0.013, 0.003 m/s.
7. **Rolling spin (Confirmed, qualified).** The formula and sign are right, but spin is slaved to velocity, not displacement, so balls pushed by the position correction slip: path length over rolled arc ranges 0.28 to 4.37 in v1 and 1.00 to 1.01 in v3.
8. **Scale (Suspected).** The ball radius 0.0969 m is about 3.4 times a real pool ball (from memory, not checked against a source); the 0.35 m/s^2 rolling deceleration is not a calibrated value.

## v2 (jelly, cloth, water)
1. **Floating ball "0.019 vs line theory ~0.03" is wrong (Confirmed).** The exact sphere float line is -0.016 m (-0.010 against the mean level); the ~0.03 is a vertical-prism estimate. The simulated 0.0187 is the model's own equilibrium: the 16-cell footprint is 12% small, which lifts the float line by about 0.029 m. The agreement was a coincidence of two errors. Heave period 0.80 to 0.83 s is sane.
2. **Wave blow-up explanation "the wave outruns a cell per step" is refuted (Confirmed).** c dt / dx is 0.83, under one cell. The cause is the checkerboard grid mode of the 5-point Laplacian under symplectic Euler: stability needs c^2 dt^2 lambda_max <= 4, i.e. c dt / dx <= 0.7082 on the 28x28 grid (1/sqrt 2 = 0.7071 in the infinite grid; 1/sqrt d in d dimensions).
3. **Stability scan numbers (Confirmed).** "1.2e6 at 0.72" is the value at the first crossing of 1e6 (the scan stops there), not the value after 3 s (1e30 at 0.72, 5e78 at 0.8). The calm/explodes verdict stands; the stable entries are genuine.
4. **Jelly: "a flat tet has all edges fine and zero volume" is false (Confirmed).** Six edge lengths fix the volume (Cayley-Menger). What edges cannot see is the mirror image: reflecting the apex through the base keeps all six lengths and flips the sign of the volume. Only a signed-volume constraint penalises inversion.
5. **"Substeps cheaper than iterations" (cloth) was not tested by the demo** (it varies substeps 2 vs 20, a 10x cost difference). The reviewer's equal-cost test (20 sweeps per frame) supports the claim: 20 substeps x 1 iteration peak stretch 0.173; 10x2 0.236; 5x4 0.241; 4x5 0.267; 2x10 0.387; 1x20 1.96 (Confirmed).

## v3 (witness, MuJoCo, fixed billiards)
1. **Errata section 1** (see v1 items 1 and 2): kinetic energy cannot tell the bug from the fix; the exchange test can. On an oblique hit with one ball at rest the fixed rule gives perpendicular outgoing velocities (dot -2e-16).
2. **The head-on pair reference is underpowered (Confirmed).** The geometry is axis-aligned, so every dot product is exact; a wrong normal, a missing tangential term or a wrong correction direction would still pass. `pair_run` still uses `np.linalg.norm` and `@`: on 300 random oblique pairs, 19 (6%) had differing per-step hashes on a pure-float host. The errata's "explicit sqrt" claim holds for the bead only. Needed: an oblique exact reference (tangential unchanged, normal swapped, 90 degree rule).
3. **Bounce-toll accounting (Confirmed).** The numbers 14.09 and 14.11 reproduce, but the mechanism is mislabelled: the explicit ball's 21 events are 11 floor hits (+30.28) and 10 ceiling hits (-16.17); reflection at a ceiling removes energy. The equality is a net of two signs. The accounting identity dE = N(-+g^2 h^2 / 2) + sum of jumps is exact (-5.162022 and +33.355962).
4. **Bead "0.6% period error" and "1% energy left" are one effect, and the control has no positive twin** (Confirmed). A test that always fails would also pass it. Add the 100-substep energy retention as a passing reference.

## v4 (hashing, smoke, double pendulum)
1. **"Chaos is not the problem; 5 cm for 9.8 s" is refuted as worded (Confirmed).** Both integrators are accurate (RK4 error 9.4e-9 m at 3 s), but the 9.8 s horizon is set by chaos acting on a 1e-9 model difference (MuJoCo's body inertia 1e-9 against the point-mass reference): a twin RK4 run with theta1 shifted by 1e-9 separates by 5 cm at 9.82 s. The horizon is ln(tolerance / initial difference) / lambda with lambda about 2 per second (e-fold 0.5 s): 8.9 s for 1e-9, 5.5 s for 1e-6, 2.0 s for 1e-3.
2. **Hash control caption is wrong by a factor of two (Confirmed).** The control cell was 0.30 of a ball diameter, not 0.6; 2997 missed pairs is the real number for that cell. True 0.6 diameter misses 1057 (49 checks); 0.5 misses 1884; 0.75 misses 195; 0.9 misses 22; 0.975 and 1.0 miss 0.
3. **The flux check is not a second reference (Confirmed).** With closed top and bottom it is an identity of the same arrays as the divergence (difference 7e-16), so the two limits are one measurement viewed twice.
4. **MuJoCo energy drift 3.4e-6 in the receipt is a measurement artefact (Confirmed).** Under RK4 the stored energy comes from the last stage; calling `mj_forward` before reading gives 8.86e-9, the same as the reference.
5. **Wording.** "54 sampled substeps" should read 49; "files are 2 to 4 MB" should read 0.1 to 4 MB.
6. **The witness wording "limits written before the run" cannot be verified (Confirmed).** `declared_limit` and `limit` are written by the same run from the same value, and there was one commit. The witness did behave as described: PASS, and 11 of 11 mutation controls caught. From v5 on, the pages say "limits in tmp5.py", and the fresh-process replay is described as detecting only nondeterminism over the first seconds of each run.

## What held up
Spatial hash exactness at cell size at least a diameter (0 missed, 0 false, 2.8% of pairs); the XPBD substep numbers (0.015 / 0.072 / 0.815 m); the Lagrangian pendulum equations and energy; MuJoCo against RK4 to 1.3e-7 m over 3 s; the fluid projection itself; the 2D CFL limit; the volume-constraint gradients and compliance handling in the jelly and cloth; the shadow-energy numbers (9e-13); the `np.linalg.norm` 1-ulp finding; the bisected CFL 0.7084.
